Five everyday privacy steps

Choose a topic and work through its checklist at your own pace. Small, steady steps add up.

1. Passwords

Strong, unique passwords and a password manager.

2. Two-factor authentication

A second lock on your important accounts.

3. Data-broker opt-outs

Remove your info from people-search sites.

4. Private browsing

Browse without saving history on your device.

5. Phone & email hygiene

Keep your number and inbox cleaner.

Suggested order

  1. Passwords — password manager + unique passwords
  2. Two-factor authentication — email first, then carrier and bank
  3. Phone & email hygiene — carrier PIN, secondary number, separate emails
  4. Data-broker opt-outs — bigger time investment, do after the basics
  5. Private browsing — use when it fits (shared devices, one-off searches)
