3. Data-broker opt-outs

← Back to home

What data brokers are

Data brokers are companies that collect and sell personal information — names, addresses, phone numbers, relatives, and more. People-search sites let anyone look you up. Opting out removes your listing from many of these sites.

What to do

  • Start with the biggest sites. Common ones include Whitepages, Spokeo, MyLife, Intelius, and BeenVerified. Each has an opt-out page, usually linked at the bottom under "Opt out," "Privacy," or "Do not sell my info."
  • Expect to repeat yourself. Most sites make you verify by email or phone, and listings can come back over time. Check again every 6–12 months.
  • Use a removal service if you want help. Paid services (e.g., DeleteMe, Optery, Incogni) contact brokers for you. They cost money but save time. Free DIY opt-outs work too — they just take longer.
  • Be realistic. Opt-outs reduce what's easy to find, but they can't erase everything. Government records and public filings stay public.

Data-broker checklist

  • Search your name + city on a few people-search sites to see what's listed
  • Opt out of Whitepages, Spokeo, MyLife, Intelius, and BeenVerified
  • Save confirmation emails from each opt-out
  • Check again in 6–12 months to see if listings returned
  • Consider a paid removal service if DIY feels like too much
  • Don't panic if some listings remain — opt-outs reduce, not eliminate
